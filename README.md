# BrewOps

BrewOps is brewery management software designed with a "team first" mindset and focused on making the software usable for employees at every stage of the brewing process from material recieving to handing packaged material off to the taproom and sales team.  

Instead of being based on a single device, or a paper trail throughout the process, BrewOps is designed so that every employee with access to a smartphone, tablet, or computer can track, modify, and add data to ensure time and energy is spent creeating the best product possible, and not trying to untangle a mystery or tracking down notes.

Craft breweries rely of paper trails, dry ease boards, notes on masking tape, and a million other ways to track progress when every person has a device in their pocket that could be used, making that information instantly availiable to anyone who needs it, whether thats to see an estimated date when the product is ready for release, or just to see if there's any issue that needs to be resolved.

BrewOps is designed to make sure your employees are spending more time in the cellar, and less time having to track down data for you.  Sales teams no longer have to ask atound for progress of limited release beers, production managers can instantly see an overview of what the upcoming brew schedule may need to be.

v1 features:

Recipe creation
materials inventory (from recieving to use)
Brewing, Transfer, Packaging schedule
Tank usage and estimated availability
Fermentation tracking

future:

integration with external CRMS
integration with smart devices to track temperatures and control glycol temperatures
predicative scheduling based on past sales and current inventory trends
